I'm a painter and screenwriter based in Prague. I work in large oils, drawn to Mexican themes, solitude, our relationship to the environment and the inner world, and lately to men's softness and the way we hold each other.

Before painting took over, I trained in screenwriting at FAMU and wrote for television. I still tell stories, just with drips now.
