Painter · Prague

I'm a painter based in Prague, working in oils and pastels, often on large canvases.

Paintings

I paint what I see. I combine oils with pastels.

Most of the people in my paintings have their backs to me. I never find out what they are looking at. If you ask someone on a long-distance bus what they're watching out the window, they usually shrug and say nothing, just the road.
