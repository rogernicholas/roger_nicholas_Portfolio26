Profile

I tell stories in three media: scripts, oil paint and code. I hold an MgA. in Screenwriting and Dramaturgy from FAMU, have written for Czech television and independent film, paint large-format figurative canvases, and build responsive websites in JavaScript and React. The same instincts run through all three: structure, rhythm and a clear sense of who is on the other side of the screen or the canvas.
